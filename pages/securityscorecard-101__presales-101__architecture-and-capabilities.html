Architecture and capabilities

Two platforms, one sign-in

PlatformAddressWhat lives there
SecurityScorecard platformplatform.securityscorecard.ioScorecards, score factors, digital footprint, portfolios, Rule Builder alerts, Reporting Center, questionnaires, action plans, advanced admin
TITAN AIapp.securityscorecard.ioAssess (intake, templates, assessments), Secure (findings, events, fourth parties, remediation), workflows, alerts, Communication Center, My Company

TITAN AI runs alongside the existing platform. Nothing in the existing platform is switched off. The navigation shows only the products the customer bought.

One workflow from intake to remediation

Intake, Scope (risk-level rules), Assess, Monitor, Remediate, all driven by AI agents, SecurityScorecard threat intelligence and workflow automation.

My Network and My Company

  • My Network is the customer's view of its own vendors. It shows their profiles, findings, events and the assessments the customer sent them.
  • My Company is the same customer, seen as a vendor. It shows the customer's own profile and events. It also shows the questionnaires and requests that the customer's own customers send.

Data behind the ratings

securityscorecard.com states these figures as of October 2026:

  • 4.1 billion IPs and domains scanned every day.
  • More than 100 billion vulnerabilities and attributions published every week.
  • More than 7 billion leaked credential and PII records.
  • 27 billion data points collected every week.
  • 10 years of history.

SecurityScorecard acquired Driftnet in May 2026. Driftnet's internet-scanning engine now feeds into TITAN AI. The October 2026 deck lists FedRAMP, StateRAMP and TX-RAMP certifications. The website states SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliance.
