Reference architecture

The reference architecture has one governance layer on top of four capability blocks.

Click a block for the next level of detail.

Orchestration and governance

Unified risk view and MTTN · autonomy policy · ITSM, SIEM, SOAR · automated response

Outside-in: third-party risk (SecurityScorecard)

  • TITAN Watch
  • TITAN Assess
  • TITAN Secure
  • TITAN MAX

Outside-in: own external exposure (SecurityScorecard)

  • Self-Monitoring and Attack Surface Intelligence
  • STRIKE threat intelligence
  • Compliance mapping
  • CRQ risk quantification

Inside-out: CTEM and xSPM (Mondoo)

  • CTEM five stages, three AI agents
  • ITSM: 8 integrations; alerts: 7 channels
  • Security pipeline that opens fix pull requests
  • xSPM across 7 domains; IaC: 8 kinds
  • xgrep SAST, 24+ languages
  • MQL policies, 30+ frameworks
  • AI SPM, shadow AI, AI BOM

Adversarial: penetration testing (Alt Security)

  • Peter, the agentic hacker
  • Business-logic flaws and proven attack chains
  • Fix Radius™ fix prioritization
  • Black, gray and white box
  • Web apps, APIs, AI apps (mobile and network coming soon)
  • Guardian Agent guardrails

Handoffs between the blocks

Solid arrows carry risk signals and evidence up to governance. Dotted arrows carry policy, approval and automated response back down.

FromToWhat is passed

Alt Security proofMondooFix tickets with the proven attack path

All threeGovernanceRating, compliance status and attack-validation results as one evidence flow

How the blocks work together

  • SSC shows what the outside world sees, about your suppliers and about you.
  • Mondoo shows what is inside and fixes it through tickets and pull requests.
  • Alt proves which exposures are exploitable and re-tests after a fix.
  • The governance layer joins the results into one risk view, applies the autonomy policy, and reports MTTN.

Counts above are as of the October 2026 materials.
