Deployment and onboarding

Timeline

Onboarding runs about 0 to 45 days, with go-live around day 30 and a hypercare period of about 30 days.

PhaseWhat happens
1. Kick-off and getting startedIntroductions, use cases and success metrics, onboarding plan; 1 to 3 Q&A sessions
2. Configuration and workshopsPlatform configuration; onboarding workshops (the sessions below); 1 to 2 review sessions
3. Practical applicationThe customer does one real task end to end; go-live
AfterHypercare, then customer success: insight reviews and executive business reviews

The seven sessions

SessionTopic
0 Kick-offTeam, license, use cases, plan, resources
1 Getting started and vendor intakeUsers and roles, vendor directory, intake form, risk-level rules
2 Assessing vendors (Assess)Templates and scoring, creating and sending assessments, AI pre-fill and evaluations, review
3 Monitoring with SecureFourth parties, findings and presets, vendor responses, security events, dashboard and alerts
4 Workflows and automationTriggers and blocks, templates, AI blocks, test and publish
5 My Company and vendor responsesOwn profile, events, remediation requests, questionnaires, knowledge base
6 Practical application and reviewCustomer scenario, guided practice, onboarding review, hypercare

Who is involved

Account Executive, Customer Success Manager, Solutions Architect, Onboarding Lead and Customer Support, from SecurityScorecard.

First tasks for the customer admin

  1. Sign in at app.securityscorecard.io through SecurityScorecard sign-in.
  2. Assign roles. The three roles are Org Admin, Editor and Viewer. Roles do not carry over for users migrated from the SecurityScorecard platform. A migrated user starts with no role.
  3. Load vendors (add directly, import CSV or intake form) and group them.
  4. Publish the intake form with an allowlist of emails or domains.

Hand over with Operations handover.
