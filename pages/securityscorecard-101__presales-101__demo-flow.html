Demo flow

Pick the scenario that matches the customer's day. Each is a real task from start to finish in the platform.

Scenario 1: a new vendor arrives (Intake, Assess)

  1. Share the intake form. The requester needs no account.
  2. Show the risk level calculated from the answers. The first matching rule wins.
  3. Approve: the vendor lands in the directory with details filled in.
  4. Open the vendor profile: score, findings, events, products used.

Scenario 2: annual reassessment (Assess)

  1. Reuse a template (CAIQ Lite or SIG Lite) or auto-import the customer's spreadsheet.
  2. Pre-fill with AI and send to the vendor.
  3. Show AI evaluations flagging risky answers. Review only the failing ones.

Scenario 3: a breach hits the news (Secure)

  1. Open Events and see which vendors are named victims or detected connections.
  2. Apply a preset such as Actively Exploited CVEs.
  3. Flag it, request remediation, or accept the risk. Track the vendor's response.
  4. Show the rescan that reopens a finding that is not really fixed.

Scenario 4: a customer asks about you (My Company)

  1. Triage the event as impacted or not impacted.
  2. Import their questionnaire and autofill from the knowledge base, with sources shown.

Scenario 5: automate a routine (Workflows)

  1. Start from a template. One example routes vendor intake by risk.
  2. Dry run, fix errors, publish, add an alert.

Scenario 6: AI agents and the unified risk view

  1. Open a scorecard and show the unified risk view: Security Score, Breach Susceptibility Indicator and Ransomware Score in one place.
  2. Ask ChatSSC for the three most urgent issues. Use an agent to draft a remediation plan.

Tie it to PEM

End the demo with the loop: see it, prioritize it, ask for the fix, confirm it. Then show the handoffs: Critical assets can go to Inside-out exposure (Mondoo) and Adversarial validation (Alt Security). See Closed-loop operating flow.
