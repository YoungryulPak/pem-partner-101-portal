How to choose a vendor

Choose by perspective first, then by outcome. The perspectives come from PEM and the four perspectives.

Step 1: whose exposure, seen from where?

If the customer's worry is...PerspectiveVendor
"Our suppliers could get us breached"1 TPCRMSecurityScorecard (TITAN Watch, Assess, Secure, MAX)
"We don't know what the internet sees of us"2 Own outside-inSecurityScorecard (Self-Monitoring, Attack Surface Intelligence, STRIKE)
"Our cloud, servers, code or AI tools are messy inside"3 Inside-outMondoo
"Is that finding actually exploitable?"4 Pen-test validationAlt Security

Step 2: what outcome do they need?

OutcomeLook at
See it: visibility and ratingsSecurityScorecard (perspectives 1 and 2)
Fix it: tickets, pull requests, policy enforcementMondoo (perspective 3)
Prove it: exploitability and before-and-after evidenceAlt Security (perspective 4)
Report it: audit, board, insurer, regulatorCompliance evidence from all three; see Evidence roles by vendor

Step 3: who will run it?

Operating modelOption
Customer team runs itTITAN Watch, Assess, Secure; Mondoo platform; Alt continuous testing
Wants the whole workflow automatedTITAN Secure with Workflow and Agents; Mondoo AI agents
Wants operations outsourcedTITAN MAX (certified partner); Mondoo Managed Vulnerability Service

Per-vendor signals

VendorChoose it when you hearDo not expect it to
SecurityScorecardSupplier risk, questionnaires, "what is exposed outside", KEV and new CVE impact, board or insurer reportingFix configuration or patch inside your infrastructure (that is Mondoo); prove exploitability (that is Alt)
MondooCloud misconfiguration, IaC, vulnerability backlog, Shadow AI, CI/CD supply chain, continuous PCI DSS, NIST or ISO checksRate your suppliers, or give an outside-in view of the internet (that is SecurityScorecard); prove exploitability (that is Alt)
Alt Security"Prove this is exploitable", business-logic flaws in a core web app, retest after a fix, MTTN evidenceRun continuous inventory or compliance (that is Mondoo and SecurityScorecard)

Common combinations

SituationCombinationWhy
Supplier-heavy, regulated customerSecurityScorecard only to startPerspective 1 with Watch and Assess. Add Secure later
Wants to see and shrink own exposureSecurityScorecard (2) + Mondoo (3)SecurityScorecard finds it from outside. Mondoo finds the owner and fixes it
Needs proof for critical findingsAdd Alt Security (4)Exploit proof feeds Mondoo tickets. A retest then confirms the fix
Wants the full closed loopAll four perspectivesSee the Closed-loop operating flow

Quick check for the customer conversation

  1. Which of the four perspectives hurts most today?
  2. Is the problem seeing, fixing or proving?
  3. Who will operate it: the customer, a partner or the vendor?
  4. Which frameworks must be evidenced? See Framework coverage matrix.
