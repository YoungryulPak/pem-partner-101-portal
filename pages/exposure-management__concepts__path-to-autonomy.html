Path to autonomy

PEM does not switch on all at once. Automation grows in steps. Trust grows in steps too.

PhaseTypical timingWhat it looks like
Workflow orchestrationNowTickets created, enriched, tracked and closed automatically
Agentic1 to 3 yearsAI agents take actions such as containment and configuration fixes
Domain-specialized3 to 8 yearsSpecialist models and cooperating agents
Autonomous8+ yearsSelf-directing defense across domains

Phases and timing are Gartner's outlook (G00845340).

What to tell a customer

  • Start small. Automate the fixes that are low risk and well understood first.
  • Preview before acting. Show the impact of an automated change before it runs. Use a semi-automated mode before a fully automated one.
  • Keep a human switch. People can turn approval on or off at any step. People can also see why an agent acted.
  • Tier by impact. High-impact changes still need approval. Low-impact changes run automatically.

These rules are the autonomy policy in the governance layer. See Governance and orchestration.

Why adoption speed differs

Some systems can be rebuilt from a known image. These systems adopt automation faster. Other systems are tightly tied to data, such as a bank ledger or patient records. These systems adopt automation more slowly. Architecture matters more than industry here.
