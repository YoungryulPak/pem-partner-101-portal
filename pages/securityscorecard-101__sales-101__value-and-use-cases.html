Value and use cases

SecurityScorecard: what it sells

SecurityScorecard's TITAN AI platform covers two things. The first is third-party cyber risk management, often called TPCRM. The second is the customer's own external exposure. The platform starts from outside-in security ratings. It adds AI agents for questionnaires, threat-informed monitoring and workflow. TITAN MAX is a managed option. It runs the program for the customer.

Why customers care

  • Third parties are a leading path for attackers. Vendor counts keep growing, but teams do not.
  • Teams cannot tell which vendor or finding to handle first. Teams also cannot trust stale or self-reported data.
  • Attackers use AI for reconnaissance, so a once-a-year review is too slow.

Where customers start: the TPRM maturity curve

StageWhat it looks likeTITAN fit

Continuous TPRMSteady oversight and risk-reduction controlsTITAN Secure
Threat-informed TPRMProactive fixing of supplier issuesTITAN Secure, TITAN MAX

The October 2026 deck adds rough markers for each stage. One marker is the share of vendors monitored, from 0% up to 100%. Another marker is the share of critical issues resolved, from below 40% up to above 95%. The last marker is time to resolve, from over 90 days down to under 30 days.
