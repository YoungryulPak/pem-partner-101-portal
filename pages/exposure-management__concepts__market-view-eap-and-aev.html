Market view: EAP and AEV

Two platform types sit under exposure management today. Analysts expect the two types to merge into unified platforms.

TypeIn one line
Exposure assessment platforms (EAP)Continuously find and prioritize exposures across external, internal, cloud and end-user surfaces, then help get them fixed
Adversarial exposure validation (AEV)Prove which exposures can be exploited, using automated penetration testing and attack simulation
Gartner published its first Magic Quadrant for Exposure Assessment Platforms on 10 November 2025 (G00808226). Gartner expects EAP and AEV platforms to add mitigation and remediation over time. As a result, standalone EASM, CAASM and control-assessment tools are expected to fade into these larger platforms (G00845340).
