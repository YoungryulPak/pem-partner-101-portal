Architecture and capabilities

One platform, one flow

Scan every layer, model it in one inventory, check it with policy-as-code, rank it, route it, fix it, and prove it by rescan.

StepComponent
ScanOpen-source scanner, agent-based or agentless, across infrastructure, workloads, build and identity
ModelUnified inventory: every asset in one data model
CheckPolicy engine: policy-as-code in MQL
RankPrioritization by exploitability and business context
RouteWorkflow engine: routing, exceptions, approvals
FixThree AI agents; delivery through GitOps, MDM and ITSM

The five CTEM stages

StageWhat Mondoo does
ScopingSpaces, organizations and asset groups; coverage assessment against the CMDB and network
DiscoveryLinux (15+ distributions), Windows, macOS; cloud on 10 platforms; 10+ SaaS apps; containers and Kubernetes with an operator and admission control; 78 Active Directory checks; eight IaC tools; xgrep SAST for 24+ languages; AI discovery
PrioritizationRisk score from five tunable dimensions (attack surface, blast radius, business priority, exploitability, news trends); third-party imports into one queue; attack-path analysis on an asset graph
Validation350+ CIS benchmarks, BSI IT-Grundschutz, DISA-STIG; 30+ frameworks; continuous compliance with cross-framework mapping
MobilizationThree AI agents; tickets, pull requests and MDM; drift detection; SLA management

Nine analyst categories in one platform

CTEM, vulnerability management, CSPM, CNAPP, SSPM, ISPM, ASPM, KSPM and AI security posture. Seven are cross-domain posture (xSPM) domains.

Three AI agents

AgentRole
Prioritization agentRanks by exploitability, blast radius and business context
Orchestration agentRoutes each fix to the right team, tool and workflow
Remediation agentGenerates fixes in Ansible, Terraform, Kubernetes manifests and shell scripts and delivers them through GitOps and MDM
