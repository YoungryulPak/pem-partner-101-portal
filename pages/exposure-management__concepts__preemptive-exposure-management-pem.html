Preemptive exposure management (PEM)

PEM is the starting point of every perspective in this portal. This portal covers four perspectives: third-party risk, your own outside-in exposure, the inside-out view, and penetration-test validation. All four are one lifecycle, seen from four angles.

In one paragraph

PEM automates the whole exposure lifecycle with AI. Exposures are closed before they are used. PEM moves people from in-the-loop to on-the-loop. In-the-loop means a person does each step. On-the-loop means a person sets policy and supervises. PEM also changes how success is measured. It uses MTTN, mean time to neutralize, instead of time to patch.

The six processes

ProcessQuestion
DiscoverWhat do we have, and who are we connected to?
AssessWhat is wrong with it?
PrioritizeWhat matters first?
ValidateCan it really be exploited?
RemediateWho fixes it, and how?
MitigateHow do we stop it coming back, and cover the wait for a fix?

Three ideas to remember

  1. Block, don't just observe. A dashboard reports. PEM acts.
  2. Automate the fix, inside rules. People define an autonomy policy. The policy says what runs automatically, what needs approval, and what never runs by itself.
  3. Measure neutralization. A patch is only one way to neutralize an exposure. MTTN counts the time until risk is actually reduced.
Gartner introduced preemptive exposure management in a report called "Emerging Tech: The Future of Exposure Management Will Be Preemptive" (G00845340, 13 Feb 2026). This page explains the same ideas in the portal's own words.
