Lifecycle

PEM runs six processes in a loop. Gartner's point is that all six processes must be automated, not just the first few. See Preemptive exposure management (PEM) for that argument, and CTEM: the program method for the CTEM view. Each stage is automated with AI and supervised by people. Click a stage in the diagram, or in the list below the table, for a detail page.

#StageQuestion it answersMain contribution
1DiscoverWhat do we have, and who are we connected to?SSC finds third and fourth parties and internet-visible assets; Mondoo inventories infrastructure, apps and AI; Alt learns application structure
2AssessWhat is wrong with it?SSC grades suppliers A-F and scores own exposure; Mondoo assesses vulnerabilities, configuration, identity, code, AI security and compliance; Alt assesses attack paths
3PrioritizeWhat matters first?SSC aligns to real threats (STRIKE, KEV); Mondoo ranks by five risk factors; Alt orders by proven attack chains
4ValidateCan it really be exploited?Alt proves exploitability; SSC and Mondoo add supporting evidence and rescans
5RemediateWho fixes it, and how?Mondoo opens tickets and pull requests; SSC requests and tracks supplier action
6MitigateHow do we stop recurrence?Mondoo enforces policy and prevents drift; SSC and Mondoo automate response workflows

Human role

  • In-the-loop (before): a person runs discovery, triage, tickets and follow-up.
  • On-the-loop (PEM): a person sets the autonomy policy and reviews outcomes; systems perform the steps.
