Closed-loop operating flow

The three solutions form one loop. Exposure found by one is prioritized, proven, fixed and re-checked by the others.

01 DiscoverThird parties and own external exposure (SSC); infrastructure and app assets (Mondoo); app structure (Alt)
02 PrioritizeThreat signals (SSC); five risk factors (Mondoo); attack chains (Alt)
03 ValidateCheck whether issues on critical assets can be exploited (Alt)
04 RemediateSupplier external exposure (SSC); ticket or pull-request workflow with approval, then fix (Mondoo)
05 Re-validateRe-attack and rescan to confirm neutralization (SSC, Mondoo, Alt)
06 MeasureReport MTTN and grade change (governance layer)

Example walk-through

  1. SecurityScorecard reports an exposed service and a newly exploited CVE that affects it.
  2. Mondoo finds the owner. Mondoo also finds the configuration or package behind the exposure. Mondoo ranks it against the other risks it knows about.
  3. Alt Security tests whether the service can be exploited. Alt Security shows the attack chain.
  4. Mondoo opens a ticket or a pull request. The owner approves the change. The fix is applied.
  5. Alt Security attacks again. SecurityScorecard and Mondoo rescan. The exposure is confirmed neutralized.
  6. The team records MTTN for this item. The team reports the grade or score change.
