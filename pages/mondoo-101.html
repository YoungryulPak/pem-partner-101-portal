Mondoo 101

Mondoo is the inside-out view, and it ends in a fix. Mondoo finds what is wrong in your cloud, servers, containers, code, SaaS and AI tools. It ranks each finding by real risk. It drafts the fix. It proves the fix landed. Mondoo is the PEM perspective that remediates and mitigates on your own infrastructure and applications.

Value in one minute

You want toMondoo gives you
See everything you runOne inventory and one data model across servers, endpoints, cloud, Kubernetes, SaaS, network devices, code and AI, with 52 native providers
Know what mattersA risk score built from five dimensions (attack surface, blast radius, business priority, exploitability, news trends) and Initiatives that group findings by the risk they remove
Fix itThree AI agents that prioritize, route and generate fixes, delivered as pull requests, MDM actions and tickets in your own workflow
Prove the fixA rescan confirms the change; tickets reopen on drift
Stay audit-readyPolicy-as-code with 30+ frameworks and 350+ CIS benchmarks, with automatic evidence

One platform replaces the five to seven separate tools a team usually runs. Those tools usually cover cloud, endpoints, containers, code and compliance separately.

How you can run it

Mondoo offers three deployment models. All three use the same platform and the same dashboard. The first is multi-tenant SaaS. The second is a dedicated single-tenant instance, run by Mondoo. The third is Bring Your Own Account. In Bring Your Own Account, Mondoo deploys and operates the platform inside your own AWS, Azure or GCP account.

Where it fits in PEM

Mondoo is primary for discover, assess, prioritize, remediate and mitigate. It is partial for validate: Mondoo rescans after the fix. Pair Mondoo with SecurityScorecard for the outside-in view. Pair it with Alt Security for proof of exploitability and re-testing. See Inside-out exposure (Mondoo) and How to choose a vendor.
