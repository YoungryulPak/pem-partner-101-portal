Orchestration and governance

One closed loop, measured by MTTN

The closed loop runs discover, prioritize, validate, remediate, re-validate, then measure. Solid signals (risk and evidence) flow up to governance. Dotted signals (policy, approval, automated response) flow back down.

  • Unified risk view and MTTNGrade change and mean time to neutralize, reported in one place
  • Autonomy policyMoves people from in-the-loop to on-the-loop by defining what may run on its own
  • ITSM, SIEM, SOARTicket, alert and workflow integrations, plus automated response
  • Compliance evidenceSupplier rating, compliance status and attack-validation results combined into one flow

Outside-in · third parties

SecurityScorecard TITAN

Which of your suppliers could cause a breach?

Start outside your own company. Your suppliers, and their suppliers, carry risk for you. Get visibility into third and fourth parties first. Then automate assessment and response.

  • TITAN WatchContinuous A-F ratings; detects vendors you have not registered
  • TITAN AssessAI questionnaire automation; auto-fills from vendor documents
  • TITAN SecureThreat-based priority, Remediate Agent, breach triage

Orchestration and governance

One closed loop, measured by MTTN

The loop has six steps: discover, prioritize, validate, remediate, re-validate, measure. The three solutions hand work to each other through this loop. People set an autonomy policy. The policy moves people from in-the-loop to on-the-loop.

  • Unified risk view and MTTNGrade change and mean time to neutralize
  • Autonomy policyWhat may run automatically, what needs approval
  • ITSM, SIEM, SOARTickets, alerts and workflows, with automated response
  • Compliance evidenceSupplier rating + compliance status + attack-validation results in one flow
